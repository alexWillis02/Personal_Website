Selected for BYU's Formula SAE capstone R&D team, developing the
next-generation high-voltage battery box and active liquid cooling system for
the FSAE vehicle.

- Work spans the tractive HV battery, structural/CAD, and thermal analysis
  subteams, so design decisions get made across all three at once.
- Builds directly on production battery-pack work at Teledyne FLIR: cell
  selection, pack construction, and destructive validation testing.
